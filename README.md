## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🛣️ [Road Damage Detection with YOLO](https://github.com/Sultan-Haider-Cheema/road-damage-detection-yolo)
Compared **YOLOv5s, YOLOv8n and YOLOv8s** on RDD2022 (38k+ images, 5 damage classes) under identical training settings, and deployed the best model in a **Streamlit** app.
- YOLOv8s gave the best accuracy: **mAP@0.5 = 0.617**. YOLOv8n was the fastest at **~290 FPS**.
- A cross-country test (trained on Japan only) revealed a strong **domain shift**: mAP@0.5 fell from 0.60 to as low as 0.07 in other countries.

`Python` `YOLOv8` `Object Detection` `Streamlit`

### 📈 [kNN & Data Reduction on Waveform](https://github.com/Sultan-Haider-Cheema/KNN_waveform)
Tuned kNN with cross-validation, studied learning curves, and implemented **Condensed** and **Reduced Nearest Neighbour** instance selection. Benchmarked the results against Decision Tree, Random Forest and RBF-SVM.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `kNN` `Instance Selection`

### 🎒 [Empirical Evaluation of Knapsack Algorithms](https://github.com/Sultan-Haider-Cheema/knapsack_project)
From-scratch implementations of **brute force, greedy, dynamic programming, branch & bound and an FPTAS** for 0/1 knapsack, evaluated on synthetic instances and **OR-Library** benchmarks with known optima.

`Python` `Algorithms` `Optimization`

### 🎵 [Spotify Songs Data Mining](https://github.com/Sultan-Haider-Cheema/spotify-data-mining-project)
Genre classification, popularity regression and K-means clustering on 26k+ tracks in **R**. The Random Forest reached **53% accuracy** across 6 genres, against an 18% baseline.

`R` `Random Forest` `Clustering` `tidyverse`
